Works with the gear you own.

Ember talks to your lights, plugs and game libraries directly on your own network. Most need no account, and nothing is sent to the cloud unless you switch it on.

Anything OpenRGB supports

ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte, ASRock, HyperX, SteelSeries and many other brands show up in Ember when OpenRGB supports them and their own software, such as iCUE or Armoury Crate, is closed. They need no extra code in Ember.

Missing yours?

Planned and in-progress integrations are on the roadmap, and ones still being proven carry a Beta tag.

See the roadmap

Beta means written and tested in software, but not yet tried on enough real devices. Smart plugs are read only, always: Ember asks a plug what it is drawing and never switches it.
