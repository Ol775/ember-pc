The demo

Meet Ember.

Nine pages. One calm window. Scroll to watch your PC settle, then take the controls yourself.

Dashboard

Your whole PC, at a glance.

CPU, GPU, board and fans, with a ten minute history. Calm when it is calm.

Fans

One click from silent to full.

Five modes, each with a curve you can see. Safety limits stay on in every one.

Lights

Light up the whole desk.

Stack effects, paint single LEDs and watch a live preview before anything changes.

Games

A fan mode for every game.

Your Steam, Epic, GOG, Ubisoft Connect and EA app libraries, found for you.

Power

What the wall socket sees.

A read-only smart plug measures the whole PC. Ember shows you the rest.

Stream Deck

Your desk, one key away.

Lighting scenes, fan modes and live readings on the keys of a Stream Deck. Beta.

Alerts

Quiet at night. Loud when it matters.

Switch fan mode on a schedule, and get a Windows notice when a part runs hot or a fan stops. Beta.

Specs

Everything inside, on one page.

Processor, graphics card, memory, drives with health, displays and Windows. Copy it for a support request. Beta.

Make it yours.

Start with Windows, pick a theme and an accent colour, and keep the 90° safety rule always on.

Read it

Made-up numbers from a tiny model: more load makes heat, more fan takes it away. The real Ember reads your actual sensors.

Ember reads your sensors, runs your fans and lights your desk. It never sends a byte on its own, never switches your plugs, and never lets a fan stop while the CPU is hot.

90°

Every controlled fan goes to 100%, on any sensor.

5

Fan modes, plus your own curves.

13

Lighting systems, found on your network or through OpenRGB.

Accounts. Nothing leaves your PC.
