# Roadmap

## Done

- Live sensor dashboard with history
- Fan modes: Super quiet, Quiet, Normal, Performance, Extreme, and Manual curves with an editor
- Safety rules, header testing, conflict detection, watchdog service
- Lighting through OpenRGB and Govee (LAN and cloud), layered effects, scenes, screen sync
- Settings page, dark window frame, smooth scrolling
- Experimental switch for game detection and GPU fan control

## Next

- Finish verifying game detection and GPU fan control on real hardware, then take them out of Experimental
- AI mode: learns your temperatures and noise preference to suggest a curve
- Installer and signed releases, instead of building from source
- Continuous integration builds
- Faster screen capture (DXGI) and audio-reactive effects
- Per-LED editing and Govee segments

## Not planned

- Overclocking or voltage control
- Cloud accounts or telemetry
