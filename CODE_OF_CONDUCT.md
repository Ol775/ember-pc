# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. We want Ember's community to be welcoming to everyone.

- Treat people with respect. No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Give feedback that helps.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Do not share other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact Ol775 through the address on their GitHub profile.
